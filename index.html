Local LLM: Mobile LLM Server – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026. Developer: DIY-Automate. Package: dev.phonellm.

Summary

Local LLM runs language models on your phone. Your prompts, chats, documents and model answers are processed on the device and are not sent to the developer. The developer runs no servers and collects no personal data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

Data that stays on your device

  • Chats, prompts and answers, and documents you add for search (RAG), including PDFs.
  • Downloaded model files, settings, usage statistics and response-time metrics.
  • The API key for the optional local server, generated on the device.

This data is stored in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Optional local API server

If you start the server, other devices on your local network can send requests to the model on your phone. Requests need the API key shown in the app. Requests and answers travel only between those devices and your phone; they do not pass through the developer.

Model downloads

When you download a model, the app fetches it from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Hugging Face receives your IP address and standard request data as part of serving the download, under its own privacy policy: huggingface.co/privacy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner and full-screen ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent first using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later from "Ad privacy choices" in the side menu. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data you create stays in the app's private storage. Data sent by the AdMob SDK and to Hugging Face is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Traffic to the optional local server is on your own network.
